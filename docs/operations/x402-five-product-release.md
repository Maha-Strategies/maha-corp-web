they do not inspect remote sources, certify truth, or inherit editorial decisions.

adding a temperature offset to uncertainty; physical plausibility is not certified.

Maha-authored boundaries from the existing inspected corpus. It returns neither
source full text nor translations, does not claim an open redistribution license,
and refuses NC-source-dependent outputs. The narrow supported Tamil index is not
advertised as a world-religion resolver. Namesake/volcano ambiguity is retained.
This is an internal product-contract review, not legal or independent expert review.
