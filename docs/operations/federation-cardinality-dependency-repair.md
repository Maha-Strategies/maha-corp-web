# Federation cardinality and dependency repair

## Decision

Map v4 contains **1,628 route candidates** and references **32 non-route canonical graph objects**. With 2,372 observed routes, the route projection is exactly **4,000**. Total federation objects are 1,660; objects and routes are deliberately not the same count.

The 32 identities remain available to the 94 dependency records that named them. Identity resolution does not transfer evidence: Tranche 18's three evidence-ready definitions remain three, and the other 29 remain non-ready under that historical review.

## Why

The v3 additions had no rank, tranche allocation, route-specific demand, or allocation budget. Promoting them to routes would have created 4,032 projected routes. Deleting them would reopen the dependency gaps. Non-route graph objects preserve identity without displacing reviewed routes.

## Boundary

No candidate content, review, active binding, release, route, sitemap, or llms entry changed. No build or deployment ran. v2, v3, and every Tranche 13–18 artifact remain immutable historical records.
