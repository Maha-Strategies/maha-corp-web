# Book SEO Growth Tracker

This is the working record for the public book editions. The aim is to build useful, citable topic clusters one book at a time, then let Search Console evidence—not intuition alone—determine the next publication.

## Operating standard

- A page must answer a real reader question, be materially distinct from the manuscript chapter, and link back to the relevant book hub.
- Broad queries require stronger evidence, clearer terminology, and more sustained topical depth than a single article can provide. Publishing more text is not, by itself, a ranking strategy.
- Do not manufacture authority with thin pages, copied prose, or artificial links. Record source boundaries, author attribution, and the distinction between established mechanism and interpretation.

## Measurement cadence

| Checkpoint | What to record in Google Search Console | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch | URL submitted; publication date; target queries | Confirm crawl/indexing status only. Do not judge rank. |
| +28 days | Page impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, queries with at least 3 impressions | Improve title/intro or add an internal link if the page has impressions but a weak click-through rate. |
| +56 days | Changes in the same measures; new referring domains if available | Publish the next supporting article only where the data reveals a related question or gap. |
| +90 days | Best queries, pages, linking domains, and indexed-page status | Decide whether the cluster merits another deep guide or whether to move to the next book. |
