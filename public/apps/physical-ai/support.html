Help and support

Contact Maha Strategies LLC at mayone@mahastrategies.com.

Report a problem

Include app version, Android/iOS version, the screen and steps that caused the issue. Screenshots are optional. Remove private information. Never send recovery keys, credentials or payment-card details.

Using the app

Explore shows the accepted Caldera exterior. Plan compares assumptions and runs one of three simulations. Updates distinguishes illustrative evidence from demonstrated capability. Projects stores scenarios locally. Membership explains verified access when billing is configured.

Purchases and deletion

Use Restore purchases for subscriptions. A consumable project pass needs the same recovered billing account. Cancel subscriptions in your Apple or Google account. Delete saved scenarios in Projects. Use Delete billing account in Membership for a configured account; this removes verified access and does not cancel a store subscription. Contact support if the deletion request cannot finish. Store billing is unavailable in this internal build.

Limits

No real scan capture, autonomous robot operation or whole-building construction qualification is included. Read the assumptions and unresolved gates in every exported plan.
