Privacy policy

Maha Strategies LLC · Physical AI · updated 5 October 2026.

Local projects

Project names, inputs, saved scenarios and cached capability evidence are stored on your device. Project geometry and scenario inputs are not uploaded by the app. Delete individual projects or use Clear all in Projects. Uninstalling normally removes local data; operating-system backups may retain a copy. Exported files remain wherever you choose to share them.

Optional billing

When configured, billing uses Apple or Google and RevenueCat. A pseudonymous account ID, transaction identifiers, purchase history, subscription status and account recovery information are processed to verify access and prevent duplicate credits. RevenueCat may process device/app metadata and diagnostic information as described in its privacy policy. We do not receive your payment-card details. This internal build has no configured checkout.

Billing account identifiers and transaction records are linked to the billing identity. They are not anonymous data. Service providers and hosting systems may process IP addresses and request logs. Billing credentials on the device use native Keychain or Keystore-backed storage. Delete billing account in Membership requests provider deletion and removes local authorization and recovery secrets after the provider accepts the request. A failed request can be retried. Minimal receipt tombstones are retained to prevent duplicate-credit claims. Provider deletion may be asynchronous. Deployed retention periods and backup deletion rules must be finalized before paid release.

Capability updates

A configured update feed receives a network request, including your IP address and ordinary request headers. Signed updates and verification results are cached locally. A signature verifies the publisher, not physical accuracy.

Permissions and tracking

The app does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts access, and has no advertising or behavioral analytics. Third-party billing data practices still need to be declared in the store privacy forms for the actual distributed configuration.

Your choices and contact

You can use the free examples without a billing account. For support, access, deletion or privacy requests contact mayone@mahastrategies.com. Do not email passwords, recovery keys, payment-card details or confidential project files. Cancelling a subscription in your store account is separate from deleting local projects.
