Privacy, support & terms

Game data stays local

Overclock stores runs, scores, settings, campaign progress, and local play counts on your device. It does not require a game account. It has no advertisements, advertising identifiers, tracking SDK, remote gameplay analytics, chat, or user-generated content. Gameplay works offline. Local data may be included in operating-system backups where enabled.

Premium purchases

When purchases are enabled, Apple or Google processes payment. Overclock uses RevenueCat to validate and restore Premium. RevenueCat receives purchase history, an anonymous app-user identifier, and technical information such as platform, app version, and network/device information used to deliver its purchase service. Gameplay history and local play counts are not sent to RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Read RevenueCat's privacy policy, Apple's privacy policy, and Google's privacy policy for their processing practices.

Deletion and requests

Settings → Delete local game data removes runs, progress, settings, and local play counts. Uninstalling removes local app data, subject to your operating system's backup behavior. These actions do not cancel or erase store purchases. Purchase records are managed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat. Contact us for purchase-data access or deletion requests. Deleting purchase-validation records can affect restore functionality.

Permissions

Overclock uses network access for purchase services, native haptics for optional feedback, and the system share sheet when you choose to share a run. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or health data.

Terms

Overclock is an entertainment game. Output and scores are fictional, have no monetary value, and cannot be redeemed. There is no wagering or cash prize. The game is not a clinical assessment, mental-health treatment, or financial advice.

Premium is a one-time unlock on the purchasing store. It includes additional governors, campaign scenarios, custom runs, and detailed decision logs. There is no subscription. Purchases restore within the same store account; Apple and Google purchases are separate. Refunds follow the purchasing store's terms. A network connection is needed for purchase validation and restoration.
